Houjiang Liu ("Whole-jyahng Lyoo")

I'm a doctoral candidate (2021 to present) at iSchool, UT Austin. I'm currently advised by Professor Matthew Lease in the AI & Human-Centered Computing research group and as part of the UT NLP community. My research interests center on human-AI Interaction, with a focus on designing responsible AI to protect information integrity and to empower human agency in AI-assisted research.

Previously, I was a Design Researcher in CfD at the Northeastern University, advised by professor Miso Kim and collaborated with professor Paolo Ciuccarelli. I was also a part-time lecturer teaching Interaction Design in CAMD at Northeastern. Before my academic career, I was an Interaction Designer, working at JD.com and SeetaTech AI.

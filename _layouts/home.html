Houjiang Liu ("Whole-jyahng Lyoo")

I'm a doctoral candidate at iSchool, UT Austin. I'm currently advised by Professor Matthew Lease in the AI & Human-Centered Computing research group and as part of the UT NLP community. Previously, I was a Design Researcher in CfD at the Northeastern University, advised by professor Miso Kim and collaborated with professor Paolo Ciuccarelli. I was also a part-time lecturer teaching Interaction Design in CAMD at Northeastern. Before my academic career, I was an Interaction Designer, working at JD.com and SeetaTech AI.

I design responsible AI that strengthens human expertise rather than replacing it. As AI takes on more of the thinking in expert work (from journalism to science), I build and study systems that keep people's judgment, skill, and agency at the center of work.
